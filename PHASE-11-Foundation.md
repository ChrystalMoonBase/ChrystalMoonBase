## Overview

Phase 1.1 begins only after Phase 0.2 has demonstrated that the MAST-POD system works on the actual lunar surface. It cannot be planned in detail until Phase 0.2 results are available. What follows is a directional outline, not a specification.

Phase 1.1 expands the sintered floor, increases the robot fleet, and builds the energy infrastructure needed to support the processing phases that follow.

---

## Key Additions in Phase 1.1

**Expanded solar capacity**  
Additional solar installations beyond the Phase 0.2 mast curtain. The exact configuration depends on the available flat sintered area and the power requirements of Phase 1.2 processing modules.

**Larger robot fleet**  
Additional CMB-R1 units, possibly with new head variants developed based on Phase 0.2 operational experience.

**Larger sintered floor**  
The floor built in Phase 0.2 becomes the foundation for Phase 1.1 expansion. The sintering frontier moves outward continuously.

**Communication infrastructure**  
Improved Earth link. Possibly a relay satellite if direct link geometry at Peary is limiting.

## What This Phase Requires That Does Not Yet Exist

- A launch partner willing to provide additional payload capacity to the Peary rim
- Institutional support for ongoing operations and monitoring
- Validated Phase 0.2 operational data to inform the Phase 1.1 design

**This phase cannot begin without these.** We are honest about this.
