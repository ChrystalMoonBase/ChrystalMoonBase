# Phase 3 — The Spaceport
## Chrystal Moon Base

**Status:** Very long-term speculative concept  
**Licence:** CC0

---

## The Idea

The Moon's shallow gravity well — 1/6 of Earth's — means that launching from the Moon requires dramatically less energy than launching from Earth. A spacecraft that refuels on the Moon before a deep-space mission carries far less propellant from Earth. The Moon as a launch platform changes the economics of the entire solar system.

Phase 3 proposes a universal lunar spaceport — open to any rocket operator, any nation, any mission. Standard docking and refuelling interfaces. Published openly. No exclusivity.

---

## Location

The equatorial near-side zone (Oceanus Procellarum, Mare Tranquillitatis) is the proposed location. Flat terrain, no atmosphere to fight, always visible from Earth for communications, and accessible by high-speed surface transport from the polar base.

The spaceport is not co-located with the habitat base. Rockets are violent. Their exhaust creates contamination and physical hazard. Habitats and sensitive instruments must be far from active launch pads.

---

## Timeline

Phase 3 is a 22nd-century concept. It depends on Phase 2 being operational and self-sufficient, on propellant production at scale, and on demand for lunar launch services from destinations (Mars, asteroid belt) that are currently not accessible to humans.

This is included here because the direction matters — the Moon should eventually be a harbour, not just a destination. The details are for the people who will live there.
