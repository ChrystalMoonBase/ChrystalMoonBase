## The Question

If this method works on the Moon — local materials, autonomous robots, modular construction, open standards — where else does it work?

Mars has iron oxide, perchlorates, and CO₂. Mercury's poles have ice and extreme solar energy. Ganymede has water ice and a protective magnetic field. Titan has nitrogen, organics, and liquid hydrocarbon lakes. Ceres has water ice in its mantle.

The physics of in-situ resource utilisation applies to any solid body with useful materials. The governance philosophy — open, shared, no flags, no patents — applies everywhere humanity might go.

Phase 4 is not a plan. It is a question: if we do this right on the Moon, what does "right" look like everywhere else?

---

## What This Repository Contains

Phase 4 documents are the most speculative in the repository. They describe possibilities, not plans. They raise questions, not provide answers. They are included because the long-term direction of the project matters for the short-term decisions — knowing where you want to eventually go shapes how you start.

## The Honest Limit

The founder of this project is a truck driver from the Netherlands. He does not know what the surface of Titan feels like, how to build a base on Ganymede, or what legal framework will govern human settlements in the outer solar system.

He knows that the question is worth asking, that someone should be asking it with honesty and without ownership, and that writing it down openly is better than not writing it at all.

The rest is for people who know more.
