## Summary

The Moon is a world of 38 million square kilometres. A single base is a camp. A civilisation is many specialised settlements connected by transport and communication, each located where the Moon makes something possible.

This section describes the four proposed provinces of a potential lunar civilisation — not a plan, but a direction based on the Moon's own geography.

---

## The Four Provinces

| Province | Location | Proposed Role |
|---|---|---|
| Solar Capital | Peary Crater Rim, 88.63°N | Power generation, first settlement |
| Water Treasury | South Polar Cold Traps, ~89.5°S | Ice extraction, propellant production |
| Gateway Plains | Equatorial Near-Side | Earth-Moon spaceport |
| Bridge to Mars | Far-Side Mare | Deep-space departure |
