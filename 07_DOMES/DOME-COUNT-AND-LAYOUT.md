## Layout Principles

**Clustering by function:** Sleep habitats cluster together in residential zones. Farm domes cluster near the processing modules that supply their water and nutrients. Medical domes are positioned for rapid access from all residential areas via the underground network.

**Underground connectivity:** Every dome is connected to the main arterial tunnel network. The tunnel network defines the spatial organisation more than the surface dome arrangement — because all movement between domes is underground.

**Expansion from center:** Construction begins at the Pod and expands outward in rings. The first domes built are closest to the Pod. Later domes are further away. The layout must accommodate this expansion sequence without creating dead ends in the tunnel network.

**Processing module separation:** The processing modules produce noise, heat, and potentially hazardous by-products. They are located at the perimeter of the base, downwind (a concept that requires redefinition in vacuum — perhaps "away from the residential cluster") and connected to residential areas only through the underground network.

## What We Don't Know

The actual base layout cannot be determined until:
- The specific landing site and its topography are known at 1-metre resolution
- The extent of the sintered floor after Phase 0.2 and Phase 1.1 is known
- The tunnel routing decisions of Phase 1.1 have been made

This document describes design principles, not a fixed plan. The actual layout is an engineering and planning task for future phases.
