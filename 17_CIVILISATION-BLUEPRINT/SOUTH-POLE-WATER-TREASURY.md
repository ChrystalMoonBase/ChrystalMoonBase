# South Pole Water Treasury
## Chrystal Moon Base — Civilisation Blueprint

**Status:** Speculative long-term vision  
**Coordinates (proposed):** ~89.5°S, Sverdrup-Henson basin  
**Licence:** CC0

---

## Overview

The water province — ancient ice in permanently shadowed craters at the south pole, confirmed by multiple missions. The resource that enables propellant production and long-term life support.

---

## Role in the Larger System

No province functions alone. The Solar Capital needs water from the Water Treasury. The spaceport needs propellant from both. The departure gate needs everything from everyone. Each province is defined by what the Moon's geography makes naturally possible there — not by what a single organisation decided to build.

---

## What Exists Now

Nothing. No infrastructure. No mission. No confirmed decision to build anything at this location.

This document describes a potential future role for this location, based on the physical properties of the site and the logical requirements of a multi-site lunar civilisation. Whether it is ever built depends on decisions made by people not yet involved, in timescales not yet specified.
