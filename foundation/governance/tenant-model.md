# Tenant Model
## Chrystal Moon Base — Governance

**Status:** Concept proposal  
**Licence:** CC0

---

## Who Can Be a Tenant

Any organisation that:
- Commits to the open-science principle (any research conducted on base is published openly within a defined period — currently proposed as 2 years)
- Agrees to base-wide safety rules and CMB authority over shared infrastructure
- Does not seek to patent technologies developed using CMB infrastructure
- Can sustain the financial commitment of a long-term lease

Eligible tenants include: national space agencies, universities, research institutions, commercial space companies, international organisations.

No single tenant may lease more than 20% of the base dome capacity. No single nation may control more than 25% of total leased capacity. These limits exist to prevent the base from becoming dominated by any single interest.

---

## What a Lease Includes

A dome cluster lease provides:
- Specified number of domes (mix of sleep habitats, work domes, farm allocation)
- Power allocation from the base grid
- Water allocation from the base supply
- Access to underground transport network
- Access to CMB-R1 robots for tenant use during non-construction periods (by arrangement)
- Communication bandwidth allocation
- Access to processing module outputs at published rates

---

## What a Lease Does Not Include

- Ownership of any dome or underground infrastructure (CMB retains ownership)
- Any claim to the lunar surface or resources
- Exclusivity on any technology or process
- Right to modify shared infrastructure
- Override of CMB safety decisions

---

## Revenue and Use of Funds

Lease revenue goes to base maintenance, infrastructure expansion, and operating costs. Surplus revenue (if any) is reinvested in base expansion — not distributed as profit.

CMB is not designed as a for-profit enterprise. It is designed as an open infrastructure operator. The financial model must be sustainable — costs must be covered — but the mission is not to maximise financial return.

Full financial accounts are published openly. Every euro of expenditure is publicly documented.
