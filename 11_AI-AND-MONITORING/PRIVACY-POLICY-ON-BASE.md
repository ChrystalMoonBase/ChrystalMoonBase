# Privacy Policy on the Base
## Chrystal Moon Base — Governance

**Status:** Founding concept document  
**Licence:** CC0

---

## The Honest Statement

Life on the Chrystal Moon Base involves significantly less privacy than life on Earth. This is not a policy choice — it is a consequence of physics. In an environment where any person's error or any system failure can kill everyone, the only viable safety architecture is continuous monitoring of all shared systems and spaces.

Anyone who travels to the base accepts this condition. It is disclosed completely before departure.

---

## What Is Monitored

**All shared spaces — always:**
Underground tunnels, communal domes, farm domes, medical facilities, factory areas, all corridors.

Monitoring includes: position of all residents (transponder), atmosphere quality, structural integrity, access logs.

**All utility systems — always:**
Power, water, atmosphere, waste. System state, not individual usage beyond what is needed for system management.

**Robots — always:**
All robot positions, status, and activities are logged continuously.

---

## What Is NOT Monitored

**Sleep habitat dome interiors:**
The AI monitors the dome's physical integrity (pressure, temperature, atmosphere) but does not monitor what happens inside. No cameras. No audio recording. No behaviour monitoring.

This is the one private space. It is guaranteed in the governance charter and cannot be removed by any tenant or authority.

**Personal data devices:**
Personal computers, communication devices, and tablets belonging to residents are not monitored by the base AI system.

---

## Data Retention and Access

Monitoring data is retained for a defined period (TBD — likely 90 days for most data, longer for safety-relevant events). Access is restricted to base management for safety purposes and to Earth oversight teams for engineering analysis.

Residents may request logs of their own position and access data. They may not request data about other residents.

All monitoring data relevant to safety incidents is transmitted to Earth and is reviewed by the Earth-side engineering teams. This data is not published publicly without consent of affected individuals for personal data, but aggregate and anonymised safety data is published openly.

---

## The Consent Model

Monitoring is not imposed — it is consented to. Every person who travels to the base has been fully briefed on what is monitored and why. They have signed acknowledgement that they understand and accept the monitoring conditions as a prerequisite for travel.

If a resident reaches the base and decides they cannot accept the monitoring conditions, they have the right to return to Earth on the next available departure. This option is real and is not discouraged.
