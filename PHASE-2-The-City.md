## Overview

Phase 2 is the transition from a robotic outpost to a permanent human community on the Moon. It is not designed by one person. It is not funded by one nation. It grows from the infrastructure built in Phases 0 through 1.2, using materials produced on the Moon, by teams of people who inherit an open-source foundation they did not have to build from scratch.

The details of Phase 2 cannot be written today. The technology, the political context, the available energy, and the lessons of the phases before it will determine what it looks like.

What can be written is the intent: a permanent human presence on the Moon that does not depend on continuous supply from Earth, built on an open-source foundation that belongs to everyone.

---

## What the Earlier Phases Provide

By the time Phase 2 begins, the infrastructure built in earlier phases provides:

- A growing sintered basalt floor — stable, radiation-tested, load-bearing
- Continuous solar power from the mast array
- A robot fleet capable of construction, maintenance, and repair
- Oxygen, water, and basic construction materials from Phase 1.2 processing
- A communication and navigation infrastructure
- An open-source knowledge base documenting everything that worked and everything that failed

---

## This Is Not the End

Phase 2 is not the destination. It is the point at which the Moon becomes capable of contributing to the next step — whatever that step turns out to be.
