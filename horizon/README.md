## One-paragraph summary

The four *peaks of (near-)eternal light* on Peary's rim each carry an **energetically
independent Pod**. They are linked only for **communication/coordination** (not energy),
forming a self-healing mesh. This solves shadow downtime by *spreading locations*, with
no energy transported across the crater — which is physically impossible at the required
scale (130 kW over 100 km is 2–3 orders beyond the DARPA/China/Caltech state of the art;
diffraction is a physical law). Any cabling is made **on site** from regolith: aluminium
conductor (MRE/anorthite), glass/ceramic insulation (same regolith), laid underground by
the **CMB8LF-B1** — a new *Bore/Burrow* class robot that melt-bores a smooth glass duct
and lays the cable during production. One feedstock, three functions.

## Honesty note

This chapter is intentionally split into three feasibility categories:
**(1) impossible by physical law** (e.g. 100 km laser power beam),
**(2) possible but heavy/expensive** (e.g. hauling 100 km of cable from Earth),
**(3) possible and feasible** (e.g. ISRU cable + underground melt-bored duct).
All material/physics claims are sourced; see §10 of the main chapter.
