## What Phase 2 Might Be

If all preceding phases succeed — and that is a very large "if" — Phase 2 is the establishment of the first permanent human community on the Moon.

Not a research station. Not a temporary outpost. A community of people who live there, work there, sleep there, grow food there, and raise the next generation of lunar engineers and scientists there.

---

## 500 Residents

The Phase 2 planning target is 500 permanent residents. This number is not arbitrary — it is the minimum community size that provides meaningful redundancy in essential skills (medicine, engineering, agriculture, maintenance) and a psychologically sustainable social environment.

500 people is a small town. It is large enough to have specialists, teachers, medical staff, and social roles. It is small enough to be managed as a coherent community.

---

## What Has to Be True for Phase 2 to Happen

1. All processing modules are operational and producing at scale
2. At minimum 50–60 sleep habitat domes are built and pressure-tested
3. Farm domes produce at least 20% of nutritional requirements
4. Medical facility is fully operational
5. The underground network connects all domes to both surface access facilities
6. Life support systems have been validated at scale over a minimum operating period
7. CMB governance framework is operational with active tenant participation
8. Earth supply chain is established for the materials that cannot yet be produced locally

None of these conditions exists today. Phase 2 is a direction, not a plan.

## The People Who Will Build It

The people who will actually design, engineer, and inhabit Phase 2 have almost certainly not been born yet. Phase 2 is a 21st-century project — possibly a late 21st-century project.

This document exists to give those future people a starting point. They will disagree with much of it, improve all of it, and build something better than what is described here. That is exactly the right outcome.
