# Phase 1 — Foundation
## Chrystal Moon Base

**Status:** Long-term concept — requires Phase 0.2 success first  
**Licence:** CC0

---

## What Phase 1 Is

Phase 1 is the scaling phase. Phase 0.2 proves the concept with one pod. Phase 1 asks: how do we go from a small sintered platform to an infrastructure capable of supporting human habitation?

Phase 1 spans multiple sub-phases across an estimated timeline of the 2040s–2060s. It requires institutional partnerships that do not yet exist, launch capabilities beyond what Phase 0.2 uses, and engineering solutions to problems that have not yet been solved.

---

## Phase 1.1 — Foundation Scaling

More solar capacity deployed along the Peary rim. More robots. A larger sintered floor. The Main Arterial Tunnel begins — the backbone of the underground network.

**Prerequisite:** Phase 0.2 success. Demonstrated sintering quality. Functional robot fleet. Laser power system operating reliably.

**Realistic earliest horizon:** 2040s. Only if Phase 0 demonstrators succeed, institutional partners join, and significant funding is secured.

---

## Phase 1.2 — Resource Processing

The 13 processing modules are delivered and become operational. The base begins producing glass, titanium, aluminium, and other materials from the Moon's own resources. The underground cisterns begin accumulating water. The base starts to become genuinely self-sufficient in construction materials.

**Prerequisite:** Phase 1.1 complete. Processing module engineering complete. Delivery mission funded and launched.

---

## Phase 1.3 — Autonomous Fabrication

The processing modules produce components that the M-Print module fabricates into precision parts. The first domes are printed. The base begins to manufacture itself.

**Prerequisite:** All processing modules operational. Dome printing method validated in ground testing. Structural engineering complete.

---

## What Phase 1 Cannot Promise

Phase 1 depends on things outside CMB's control: the decisions of space agencies, the progress of relevant technologies, the availability of launch vehicles, and the sustained engagement of institutional partners over decades.

The timelines above are aspirational. The sequence is logical. Whether any of it happens, and when, depends on the world — not only on this project.
