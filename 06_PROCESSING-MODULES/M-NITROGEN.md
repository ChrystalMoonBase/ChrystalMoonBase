# Nitrogen Extraction (M-NITROGEN)
## Chrystal Moon Base — Processing Module Concept

**Status:** Concept proposal — no hardware exists  
**Licence:** CC0

---

## Overview

**Input:** Lunar regolith (nitrogen present at very low concentrations — tens of ppm — implanted by solar wind)

**Output:** Gaseous nitrogen for habitat atmosphere buffer gas

**Process:** Nitrogen is extracted from regolith by thermal desorption — heating the regolith releases implanted solar wind gases including nitrogen. The quantities are small, requiring processing of very large regolith volumes per unit of nitrogen produced. Alternative: import nitrogen from Earth initially, or use argon (also present in solar wind implantation) as the buffer gas.

**Honest note:** The nitrogen content of lunar regolith is very low. M-Nitrogen may be the module with the poorest input-output ratio. Whether local nitrogen production is practically viable compared to Earth import is genuinely uncertain and depends on the actual regolith concentration at the Peary site.

**Key challenge:** The very low nitrogen concentration in regolith makes the processing-to-output ratio poor. This module may require importing nitrogen initially while local production is scaled.
