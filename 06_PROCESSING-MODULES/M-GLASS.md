## Overview

**Input:** Silicon dioxide (SiO₂) from regolith, plus aluminium oxide and calcium oxide from co-extraction

**Output:** High-purity glass in sheet, rod, and powder form

**Process:** The lunar regolith contains 40–50% SiO₂ by mass, concentrated in plagioclase feldspar minerals. Selective mineral processing concentrates the silicate fraction. High-temperature melting (>1400°C) produces glass melt, which is cast or drawn into the required form.

**Why first:** Glass is the primary transparent structural material for dome construction. Without glass, no dome can be built. No dome means no human habitation. M-Glass is therefore the first module needed after the sintering floor exists.

**Key challenge:** Achieving consistent optical and structural quality in glass produced from variable-composition regolith without the quality control infrastructure of a terrestrial glass factory.
