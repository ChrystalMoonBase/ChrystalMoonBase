## What Phase 2 Might Be

If all preceding phases succeed — and that is a very large "if" — Phase 2 is the establishment of the first permanent human community on the Moon.

Not a research station. Not a temporary outpost. A community of people who live there, work there, sleep there, grow food there, and raise the next generation of lunar engineers and scientists there.

---

## 500 Residents

The Phase 2 planning target is 500 permanent residents — the minimum community size that provides meaningful redundancy in essential skills and a psychologically sustainable social environment.

---

## What Has to Be True for Phase 2 to Happen

1. All processing modules are operational and producing at scale
2. At minimum 50–60 sleep habitat domes built and pressure-tested
3. Farm domes produce at least 20% of nutritional requirements
4. Medical facility fully operational
5. Underground network connects all domes to both surface access facilities
6. Life support systems validated at scale
7. CMB governance framework operational with active tenant participation
8. Earth supply chain established for materials not yet locally produced

None of these conditions exists today. Phase 2 is a direction, not a plan.
