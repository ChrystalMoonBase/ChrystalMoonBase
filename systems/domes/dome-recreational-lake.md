## Chrystal Moon Base — Psychological Health Infrastructure

## This Is Not a Luxury

This must be stated clearly at the beginning: the recreational lake dome is not a luxury. It is essential infrastructure for long-term human habitation.

Permanent residents of the Chrystal Moon Base face one of the most psychologically demanding environments humans have ever inhabited. Confined space. Limited social pool. No ability to leave. No wild nature. No rain, no wind, no seasons as they exist on Earth. These conditions cause cumulative psychological stress that, over months and years, leads to interpersonal conflict, depression, and reduced cognitive function — all documented in Antarctic winter-over studies, submarine deployments, and ISS long-duration missions.

Every Antarctic station that has succeeded long-term has invested in psychological wellbeing infrastructure — recreation rooms, social spaces, libraries, films. The most successful ones have natural elements: plants, water features, animals.

A swimming lake on the Moon is the most powerful psychological wellbeing investment the base can make. People need to swim. They need to hear water moving. They need to feel weightless in water. They need open space above them and the sense of a natural environment, however artificial its construction.

## Design

**The lake:** A body of freshwater, roughly circular or naturally shaped, set into the sintered regolith floor of the dome. Depth: 1.5–3 metres — deep enough to swim freely, not so deep as to require complex structural engineering of the sintered floor base. Surface area: as large as the dome diameter allows — target 200–500 m².

**The water:** Freshwater. Maintained at a comfortable swimming temperature — approximately 28–30°C. Heated by the base thermal management system. Chemically maintained to swimming pool quality standards without chlorine where possible — UV sterilisation and biological filtration are preferred.

**The bottom and banks:** Sintered regolith, smoothed and shaped. The banks can be shaped to create shallow areas, steps, and natural-feeling contours. No sharp edges. No loose material that would contaminate the water.

**In 1/6g:** Swimming in 1/6g is a different experience from Earth. The water still supports the body — water density does not change. But the effective weight of the swimmer is lower, so buoyancy feels stronger. Jumping from the bank produces a slow, floating arc. Diving is gentler. The experience is closer to weightlessness than Earth swimming — which may be precisely what residents need as a contrast to the rest of their environment.

## The Algae Pillars

The recreational lake dome also contains algae pillars — glass tubes of living algae rising from the lake floor or mounted around the perimeter. In the lake dome, these serve primarily:

- **Atmosphere processing** — CO₂ removal and O₂ production
- **Aesthetic and psychological value** — glowing columns of living blue-green organism in the water and around the lake create a genuinely beautiful, living environment
- **Light distribution** — illuminated algae pillars provide soft, natural-feeling light from multiple directions

The algae in the recreational dome are not primarily for harvesting — they are for the environment. Some harvest is possible and desirable (nothing wasted), but the primary function is biological atmosphere processing and psychological atmosphere creation.

## The View

Looking up from the water of the lake, through the dome ceiling:
- Inner glass layer — clear, smooth
- Water layer — faint aqueous quality to the light, slight blue tint
- Gold-coated glass — warm filtering of sunlight, similar to late afternoon light on Earth
- The lunar sky above — stars during lunar night, the filtered sun during lunar day
- Earth, visible from the Peary near-side rim — a blue marble above you while you float in a lake on the Moon

This view is unique in human experience. It is worth building.

## Biological Integration

The lake is not a dead body of water. It contains:
- **Aquatic plants** — water lilies, aquatic grasses, floating plants — providing naturalness, oxygen, and biological filtration
- **Small fish** — ornamental and small edible species if desired — not primary food production, but biological activity and psychological benefit
- **Algae** — natural integration with the pillar systems

The lake ecosystem is managed to maintain water quality and biological health. It is not a sterile pool. It is a living environment.

---

## Connection to Base Systems

All water connections through the underground network:
- Fresh water supply from base water system
- Return and overflow to water recycling
- Thermal management connection for temperature control
- No surface connections

The lake dome has a larger-than-standard floor hatch system — wide enough for small maintenance boats or equipment to be brought up from the underground network for lake maintenance.

---

## Psychological Research Value

The recreational lake dome is also a research opportunity. Long-duration habitation psychology is poorly understood at the scales and durations that CMB will eventually involve. Monitoring resident use of the lake dome — how often, for how long, what effect on mood and performance metrics — contributes to the scientific understanding of what humans need in permanent isolated habitats.

This research has direct value for future space habitation design. All data collected is published openly.
