# Farm Dome
## Chrystal Moon Base — Food Production

**Status:** Concept proposal  
**Licence:** CC0

---

## Overview

The farm dome is a larger, specialised dome dedicated to food production. Resident nutrition cannot depend permanently on Earth supply — the logistical cost and single-point-of-failure risk are unacceptable for a permanent settlement. Food production must be established before humans arrive, or in the earliest phases of habitation.

---

## Dimensions

Farm domes are significantly larger than sleep habitats — 20–40 metres in diameter — because food production requires area. The total farmed area required for nutritional self-sufficiency depends on the diet, the crop mix, and the productivity per square metre achievable in the lunar environment.

Estimates for closed-environment hydroponics suggest 30–50 m² of growing area per person for a complete diet. For 500 residents, this implies 15,000–25,000 m² of farm area — requiring multiple large farm domes or a different approach to food production.

This calculation is highly uncertain and depends heavily on the specific growing technology. It is a planning target, not a design specification.

---

## Growing Technology

**Hydroponics** — growing plants in nutrient solution without soil — is the most likely approach. It is more water- and space-efficient than soil-based farming, and avoids the dust and contamination risks of using lunar regolith as a growth medium.

**Lighting:** Farm domes have the same transparent dome structure as habitat domes, providing natural sunlight through the filtered layers. At Peary, the sun is always low on the horizon — daylight illumination through the dome ceiling is diffuse and relatively constant (no dramatic day/night cycle). Supplemental artificial lighting may be needed for crops requiring more light intensity.

**Water:** Hydroponic systems recirculate water efficiently. Water consumption per kilogram of food is much lower than soil farming. The closed-loop water system connects to the base water supply and return network through the underground connections.

**Atmosphere:** The farm dome atmosphere must support plant growth — slightly elevated CO₂ compared to habitat domes accelerates plant growth. The farm atmosphere is managed separately from the habitat atmosphere and is not directly connected.

---

## Dome Structure Modifications

Farm domes may benefit from a modified inner dome structure:
- Higher transparency (thinner inner glass layer, optimised gold coating) to maximise light transmission for plants
- Structural provisions for plant support frameworks
- Water distribution system integrated into the floor during construction

These modifications are within the basic five-layer dome architecture but require specific design decisions about the trade between light transmission and radiation protection.
