## Overview

Two freshwater aquaculture domes complement the saltwater domes. Freshwater and saltwater ecosystems are completely biologically isolated — cross-contamination would destroy both. They are in separate, independent domes with no shared water systems.

---

## Why Freshwater as Well as Saltwater

Different nutritional profiles. Different species. Different ecosystem dynamics. And critically — **redundancy**. If a disease outbreak affects one aquaculture system, the other continues operating. For a permanent base dependent on local food production, redundancy in every food system is not optional.

Freshwater species suited to closed aquaculture include:
- **Tilapia** — extremely hardy, fast-growing, tolerant of variable water quality, well-studied in aquaponics systems
- **Catfish** — bottom feeders, efficient converters of organic waste to protein
- **Carp** — very efficient, tolerant, high yield
- **Trout** — higher quality flesh, requires cooler cleaner water — more demanding but achievable

---

## Aquaponics Integration

The freshwater domes are particularly well-suited to **aquaponics** — combining fish farming with plant growing in the same water system. Fish waste (ammonia) is converted by bacteria to nitrates, which feed plants growing in rafts on the water surface or in channels fed by the fish tank water. Plants clean the water, which returns to the fish.

This further closes the biological loop — the dome produces both fish protein and vegetable food in the same system, from the same water, using less space than separate systems.

Suitable aquaponic plants: lettuce, herbs, spinach, watercress, and other leafy greens. These are among the most valuable foods for fresh dietary variety and psychological wellbeing in an isolated environment.

---

## Algae Pillars

Same concept as the saltwater domes — freshwater algae species in glass pillars:

- **Chlorella** — universal, adaptable to freshwater
- **Scenedesmus** — hardy freshwater species, good nutritional profile
- **Haematococcus** — produces astaxanthin, a powerful antioxidant, as well as protein

The algae pillars in the freshwater domes also contribute to CO₂ removal and O₂ production for the dome atmosphere.

---

## The Closed Loop

Fish → waste → bacteria → nitrates → plants and algae → food for fish and humans → fish

Water: recirculated, minimal loss, evaporation captured by dome atmosphere system.

Inputs: energy, makeup water, makeup mineral nutrients (initially from Earth, eventually from local mineral processing).

Outputs: fish protein, plant food, algae food supplement, O₂ to base atmosphere.

---

## Connection to Farm Domes

The freshwater aquaculture domes connect to the farm domes via the underground network. Nutrient-rich water from the aquaculture system can supplement the hydroponic nutrient solution in the farm domes. Organic waste from the farm domes can feed back to the fish as supplemental feed. The two systems enhance each other.
