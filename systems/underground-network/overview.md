# Underground Network — Overview
## Chrystal Moon Base — Infrastructure Concept

**Status:** Concept proposal  
**Author:** Berechja Kerkdijk  
**Licence:** CC0

---

## The Fundamental Principle

Dust is the enemy. Everything that moves, everything that connects, everything that transfers between locations — goes underground.

The dome is strong because it has no penetrations. The base is safe because nothing critical is exposed to the lunar surface. The underground network is the circulatory system of the base. The domes are organs sitting on top of it.

---

## What Goes Underground

**Everything** — all human movement, cargo, robots, utilities, maglev transport, connections to arrival/departure platforms, and emergency evacuation routes.

---

## The 20-Hectare Sintered Floor

The entire base sits on a single continuous sintered regolith floor of approximately 20 hectares — 505 metres in diameter. One unbroken surface. No seams, no joints, no weak points.

---

## Human Movement — Walking

Within the 20-hectare base, people walk. Maximum distance across: 505 m — a comfortable 5-minute walk. The underground walking network connects all domes via passages wide enough for two-way traffic, lit by LED, with luminescent algae pillars for ambience and atmosphere processing (algae are not bright enough to serve as primary lighting).

---

## Maglev — Long-Distance Only

Maglev serves connections too far to walk: base to arrival platform, base to departure platform, and eventually inter-base connections across the lunar surface.

---

## Safety — Underground as Last Resort

If any dome loses pressure, residents evacuate through the floor hatch into the underground network. The network maintains its own independent atmospheric integrity — a dome failure does not affect the underground passages.
