## Overview

**Input:** Anorthosite regolith (highland composition, rich in aluminium plagioclase)

**Output:** Aluminium metal in rod, sheet, and structural profile form

**Process:** Aluminium is extracted from plagioclase feldspar (AlSi₃O₈ and related minerals) by electrolytic reduction of aluminium oxide (Al₂O₃). The process is analogous to the terrestrial Hall-Héroult process but adapted for a regolith feedstock. The Peary rim highland terrain is specifically well-suited for aluminium extraction due to its anorthosite-rich composition.

**Note:** Aluminium is the workhorse structural metal of the base. It is used for secondary structures, dome support frameworks, robot chassis components (in later phases, when local production is available), and electrical conductors.

**Key challenge:** Aluminium electrolysis requires a molten fluoride electrolyte in terrestrial production (cryolite). Fluorine is scarce on the Moon. Alternative electrolyte systems for lunar aluminium production are a research topic.
