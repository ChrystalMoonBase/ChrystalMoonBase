## Overview

**Input:** Metal powders and filaments — primarily aluminium (M-Aluminium) and silicon/ferrosilicon (M-Silicon, M-Iron); alumina ceramic (M-Ceramics); glass (M-Glass). Titanium only where imported stock is available (not a local Peary metal).

**Output:** Precision-manufactured components in metal, glass, and composites

**Process:** Laser powder bed fusion (LPBF) and directed energy deposition (DED) for metals; glass printing by extrusion or laser heating. These are established terrestrial additive manufacturing technologies being adapted for space environments.

**Note:** M-Print closes the manufacturing loop. Once M-Print is operational, the base can produce precision components — gears, brackets, optical mounts, electronic housings — from local materials. This is the beginning of true manufacturing independence from Earth.

**Key challenge:** Powder management in lunar gravity (1/6 g) and vacuum. Loose metal powder in low gravity and vacuum behaves very differently than in terrestrial manufacturing environments.
