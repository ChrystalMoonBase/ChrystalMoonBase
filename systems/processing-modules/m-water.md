# Water Extraction and Processing (M-WATER)
## Chrystal Moon Base — Processing Module Concept

**Status:** Concept proposal — no hardware exists  
**Licence:** CC0

## Overview

**Input:** Water ice from polar cold traps (Peary crater interior and nearby PSRs), plus trace water from regolith processing

**Output:** Purified liquid water and electrolysed hydrogen and oxygen

**Process:** Ice extraction by thermal mining — heating of PSR surface causes sublimation of trapped ice, which is captured by cold surfaces and condensed. Purification removes volatile contaminants. Electrolysis splits water into hydrogen (fuel) and oxygen (atmosphere/oxidiser).

**Note:** Water accumulates gradually from two sources: trace amounts extracted during all regolith processing (a few tenths of a percent by mass), and larger quantities from the nearby cold traps. Both streams are stored underground in sintered regolith cisterns. The dome water layer is filled from this stored supply when sufficient volume has accumulated.

**Key challenge:** Quantifying the ice content and accessibility of Peary-adjacent cold traps — this data does not yet exist with sufficient resolution for engineering planning.
