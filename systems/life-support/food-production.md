# Food Production
## Chrystal Moon Base — Life Support

**Status:** Concept proposal 
**Licence:** CC0

---

## Overview

Complete nutritional self-sufficiency is the long-term target. Getting there is a gradual process — initial residents are substantially dependent on Earth supply, and local food production increases over time as farm dome area grows and crop systems mature.

---

## Technology Approach — Hydroponics

Soil-based farming is not proposed for the lunar base. Lunar regolith contains no organic matter and would require extensive processing to become a growth medium. Hydroponics — growing plants in nutrient solution — is more appropriate:

- Higher yield per square metre than soil farming
- More water-efficient (closed-loop water recycling)
- No dust or regolith contamination of food products
- Established technology with decades of closed-environment research (Antarctic stations, ISS experiments)

---

## Required Area Estimate

For a nutritionally complete diet, hydroponic farming typically requires 30–50 m² of growing area per person. For 500 residents:

- **Minimum target:** 15,000 m² (multiple large farm domes)
- **Adequate target:** 25,000 m²

This is a very large area. It will not be achieved immediately — farm dome construction proceeds in parallel with all other base construction over years. Initial residents rely substantially on Earth supply, with local production supplementing and gradually replacing imports.

---

## Crop Selection

Criteria: high caloric density per area, fast growing cycle, variety for nutritional completeness and psychological wellbeing.

Candidate crops (based on closed-environment agriculture research):
- **Wheat** — staple carbohydrate
- **Potato** — staple carbohydrate, high yield
- **Soybean** — protein and fat
- **Lettuce and leafy greens** — fast growing, psychological value (fresh food)
- **Tomatoes** — vitamin C, psychological value
- **Spirulina** — extremely high protein and nutrient density per area

No single crop provides complete nutrition. Diversity is essential both nutritionally and psychologically.

---

## Lighting

Farm domes use the natural sunlight transmitted through the dome ceiling, supplemented by artificial lighting as needed. At Peary, the sun is always at a low angle — direct sunlight intensity through the dome ceiling is lower than equatorial conditions but relatively constant. Crops requiring high light intensity may need supplemental LED lighting at additional energy cost.

The energy cost of artificial lighting is a significant factor in the farm dome energy budget — this must be included in the overall power planning.

## Restaurants and Bars — Essential Infrastructure

Food on the base is not purely functional. Restaurants and bars are essential social infrastructure — as important to the psychological health of the community as the medical dome is to physical health.

**Why restaurants are not a luxury:**
500 people in an isolated environment need social gathering points. A bar where people go after a work shift, a restaurant where groups share a meal — these are the spaces where community forms, where stress is released, where friendships develop. Without them, 500 individuals remain 500 individuals. With them, they become a community.

**What the base produces:**
- Fresh vegetables and herbs from hydroponic farm domes — harvested year-round, no seasons
- Fish from freshwater and saltwater aquaculture domes
- Algae-based protein supplements — processed into food products, often undetectable in finished dishes
- Grain crops for bread, pasta, and brewing
- Fruit — strawberries, tomatoes, and other crops that grow faster in controlled hydroponic conditions than on Earth

**Why optimised hydroponics is fast (it is the growing system, not the Moon):**
Controlled-environment hydroponics runs under near-optimal conditions continuously — no seasons, no weather, no pests, with tuned light and nutrient delivery. Crops therefore grow faster than in *seasonal outdoor* farming — but this advantage comes from the growing system and would apply equally on Earth; it is NOT caused by the Moon or by low gravity. (Low gravity's effect on plants is uncertain and is not a known benefit.) The point stands that the base can produce fresh food efficiently — just for the right reason.

**Brewing:**
Yeast, grain, water — all available locally. Beer, and potentially other fermented beverages, can be produced on the base. This is not trivial — shared social beverages have been part of human community for thousands of years.

**No money:**
Everything in the restaurant and bar is free. It is covered by the base lease. There are no prices, no bills, no hierarchy of who can afford what. Everyone eats the same food. The CEO and the technician sit at the same table.

## Mandatory Physical Activity

Physical exercise is not optional on the base. It is a health requirement, documented in every resident's arrival agreement and monitored by the base medical system.

**Why mandatory:**
1/6 gravity causes bone density loss and muscle atrophy even with the maglev sleeping ring providing nightly 1g simulation. Daytime physical activity is essential to supplement the passive countermeasures.

**What exercise looks like in 1/6g:**
Movement in 1/6 gravity is a fundamentally different experience. Jumping reaches 6× the height of an Earth jump. Running feels effortless but cardiovascular demand remains. New sports that are impossible on Earth become possible — vertical movement, long jumps, acrobatics that would be dangerous on Earth.

The base sports facilities are designed for 1/6g — not adapted from Earth designs but conceived specifically for the lunar environment. What these sports look like in practice is something the first residents will discover and define.

**The recreational lake:**
Swimming in 1/6g in the recreational lake is one of the primary physical activity options. The buoyancy experience is unique — the reduced effective weight changes the swimming experience in ways that cannot be replicated on Earth. The lake also provides the psychological benefit of open water — a rare and precious thing in an enclosed habitat.
