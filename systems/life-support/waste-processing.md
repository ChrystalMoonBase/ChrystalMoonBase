# Waste Processing
## Chrystal Moon Base — Life Support

**Status:** Concept proposal  
**Licence:** CC0

---

## Philosophy

Nothing leaves the base that could be useful. Everything that would be waste in a linear economy is a resource in a closed-loop lunar economy.

---

## Waste Streams

**Human metabolic waste:**
Urine → water recovery (see WATER-CYCLE.md), nitrogen and mineral residue → compost for farms
Solid waste → anaerobic digestion → biogas (CO₂ and methane) → farm atmosphere CO₂ supplement, organic residue → compost

**Food production waste:**
Plant material not used for food → compost or biogas → soil amendment or farm atmosphere

**Manufacturing waste:**
Metal scrap → remelted by M-Aluminium and M-Glass (and ferrosilicon recovery); titanium is not locally processed at Peary
Glass offcuts → remelted by M-Glass
Polymer waste → most polymers cannot be locally recycled; managed as a slow-accumulating waste stream

**Packaging and Earth-origin materials:**
The base should minimise materials arriving from Earth that cannot be locally recycled. Packing materials should be designed to be feedstock for base processes — e.g., polyethylene used as a hydrogen source in M-Iron hydrogen reduction.

---

## Zero Waste Target

The long-term operational target is zero net waste — all material inputs either remain as useful products in the base or are recycled back into feedstock streams. This target is aspirational and will not be achieved initially. But designing toward it from the beginning prevents the accumulation of waste streams that become unmanageable at scale.
