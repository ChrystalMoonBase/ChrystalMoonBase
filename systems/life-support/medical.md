# Medical System
## Chrystal Moon Base — Life Support

**Status:** Concept proposal 
**Author:** Berechja Kerkdijk 
**Licence:** CC0

---

## Overview

The base medical system operates on one principle: **everything that can happen on Earth can happen on the Moon, and the nearest hospital is 384,000 km away.**

There is no stabilise-and-evacuate model. Evacuation takes days to prepare and days to execute. The base medical system must be capable of handling any medical situation to full resolution — surgery, intensive care, recovery, rehabilitation — entirely on site.

---

## Two Complete Medical Facilities

### Surface Medical Dome
A full-scale medical facility in a dedicated dome on the 20-hectare floor. This is the primary medical facility for all routine and emergency care during normal base operations.

**Capabilities:**
- Full surgical suite — general surgery, orthopaedic, emergency
- Intensive care unit
- Diagnostic imaging — X-ray, ultrasound, MRI equivalent
- Laboratory — blood work, pathology, microbiology
- Pharmacy — comprehensive medication inventory, regularly rotated
- Isolation ward — for infectious illness, completely sealed from rest of dome
- Rehabilitation — physiotherapy, occupational therapy
- Mental health — consultation rooms, therapy resources
- Dental — full dental care
- Telemedicine — permanent connection to Earth specialists for consultation on any case

### Underground Medical Facility
An identical medical facility located underground, adjacent to the emergency shelters and maglev terminal. Completely independent of the surface dome — separate power, separate atmosphere, separate water supply.

**Purpose:**
If the surface dome is compromised — meteorite impact, pressure failure, or any scenario requiring dome evacuation — full medical capability is immediately available underground. Medical care does not stop because the surface is inaccessible.

The underground facility also serves residents who have evacuated to the emergency shelters — medical care is available exactly where the people are.

---

## Staffing — Tenant Responsibility

CMB builds and maintains the facilities and all equipment. Medical staffing is the responsibility of tenant organisations.

Every tenant lease agreement requires a proportional contribution to the base medical personnel pool. No organisation may lease dome space without contributing qualified medical staff. Together, the tenants maintain a complete medical team covering all specialties needed for a community of 500 people.

This model ensures:
- No single organisation bears the full staffing cost
- Medical expertise reflects the diversity of the resident population
- Staffing scales naturally as the base population grows

---

## Telemedicine

A permanent high-priority communication channel connects both medical facilities to Earth. Specialists anywhere on Earth can consult on any case in real time — subject to the 1.3–2.6 second one-way communication delay.

For complex cases, Earth-side specialists can:
- Review all diagnostic data in real time
- Observe surgical procedures via camera
- Provide guidance to on-site surgeons
- Access the patient's full medical record

The telemedicine system operates on its own dedicated communication bandwidth — it is never deprioritised in favour of other data traffic.

---

## AI Medical Assistance

The base AI assists medical staff with:
- Continuous vital sign monitoring of all residents via wearable sensors
- Early anomaly detection — flagging residents whose vitals deviate from their personal baseline before symptoms appear
- Drug interaction checking
- Diagnostic support — pattern recognition across the resident population
- Resource tracking — medication inventory, equipment status

**The AI does not make medical decisions.** It provides information, flags concerns, and supports the human medical team. All clinical decisions are made by qualified medical personnel.

---

## Medical Robots — Future Development

As robotic technology advances, medical robots may eventually assist with or perform some surgical procedures on the base. The same modular chassis philosophy that applies to construction robots — one chassis, specialised heads — could in principle extend to medical applications.

This is long-term speculation, not current planning. It is noted here because it is consistent with the overall system philosophy and because the base may exist long enough to see this technology mature.

---

## Mandatory Health Monitoring

All residents wear a continuous health monitoring device. It tracks vital signs, activity levels, sleep quality, and other health indicators. This data feeds into the base AI for population-level health monitoring.

**What is monitored:**
- Heart rate and rhythm
- Blood oxygen
- Temperature
- Activity and movement
- Sleep quality

**Privacy:**
Individual health data is accessible only to the resident themselves and to medical staff. The base AI uses aggregate anonymised population data for early outbreak detection. Individual data is never shared without consent except in a medical emergency.

**Psychological health:**
Mental health monitoring is as important as physical health monitoring on a base where psychological deterioration can cascade into safety incidents. Regular psychological check-ins are mandatory — not optional. Mental health staff are part of the base medical team.
