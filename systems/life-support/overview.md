# Life Support — Overview
## Chrystal Moon Base

**Status:** Concept proposal — life support engineering is a specialised discipline  
**Licence:** CC0

> Life support system design for permanent lunar habitation is one of the most complex engineering challenges in this entire project. This overview is a starting framework. Detailed engineering of each subsystem requires specialist expertise that is explicitly invited via GitHub contributions.

---

## The Challenge

500 permanent residents require continuous supply of breathable air, potable water, food, thermal comfort, and radiation protection — produced and recycled locally, without reliable Earth resupply.

**Target closure fractions (aspirational):**
- Water: >99% recycled
- Oxygen: >95% from local production
- Food: >50% locally grown (remainder imported initially)
- Nitrogen: local production when M-Nitrogen achieves adequate output
