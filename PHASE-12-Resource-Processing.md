## Overview

The lunar regolith contains iron, aluminium, silicon, titanium, calcium, magnesium, oxygen, and trace elements confirmed by Apollo, LCROSS, SELENE, and LRO mission data. Phase 1.2 is the industrial phase — where these materials are extracted, refined, and made available as feedstock for construction and life support.

Phase 1.2 consists of thirteen processing modules, each targeting a specific material or process. Each module is an independent engineering project that can be developed, tested, and deployed separately.

## The Hard Problem

Laboratory-scale extraction of these materials has been demonstrated for most of them. Scaling from a laboratory demonstration to an autonomous industrial process on the Moon — without human maintenance, without Earth supply chains, in a vacuum at extreme temperatures — is the hard problem of Phase 1.2.

This is not a solved problem. It is the engineering challenge that Phase 1.2 exists to address.
