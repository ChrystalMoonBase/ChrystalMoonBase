# Phase 0 — Earth Demonstrators Overview
## Chrystal Moon Base — Validation Programme

**Status:** Planning stage — no hardware exists yet  
**Licence:** CC0

---

## Purpose

Before anything goes to the Moon, everything must work on Earth.

Phase 0 is a ground-based validation programme consisting of five demonstrators. Each demonstrator targets a specific technology or system that the Phase 0.2 Pod concept depends on. Each produces honest, publishable results — whether positive or negative.

Phase 0 does not prove that the Pod will work. It proves — or disproves — that the individual components are viable. If any demonstrator fails, the finding is published openly and the concept is revised accordingly. A Phase 0 failure that reveals a fundamental problem is more valuable than a Phase 0 success achieved by hiding uncertainties.

---

## The Five Demonstrators

| # | Name | What It Tests |
|---|---|---|
| D1 | Laser Sintering | Can lunar regolith simulant be laser-sintered to structural quality at the required energy density? |
| D2 | Laser Power Beaming | Can laser power be delivered to a moving robot at the required efficiency over the required range? |
| D3 | Robot Locomotion | Can a hexapod robot with the CMB-R1 leg architecture operate reliably in simulated lunar conditions? |
| D4 | Swarm Coordination | Can 2–4 robots coordinate autonomously to sinter a defined area in the correct ring pattern? |
| D5 | Full Integration | Can all systems operate together — power beaming, robot locomotion, sintering, and swarm coordination — in a single test? |

---

## What Phase 0 Does Not Include

Phase 0 does not include:
- Any lunar hardware (lander, mast, pod structure)
- Any vacuum testing (desirable but not required for initial validation)
- Any 1/6 g testing (requires parabolic flight or specialised facility — planned for Phase 0.5 if funded)
- Any real lunar regolith (simulants are used — MMS-1, LHS-1, or equivalent)

Phase 0 is a proof-of-concept programme, not a qualification programme. It answers the question "is this physically plausible?" not "is this ready to fly?"

## Estimated Cost and Timeline

Phase 0 is the first target for crowdfunding and early institutional support.

Estimated total cost: €1.5–3 million across 3–5 years for the full five-demonstrator programme plus documentation and a scientific record of results.

This is an estimate with significant uncertainty. Actual costs depend on facility access, material costs, personnel, and whether any demonstrators require redesign after initial failures.

Crowdfunding alone will not cover this. The goal of Phase 0 crowdfunding is to fund Demonstrator 1 (the most accessible and least expensive) and to create a credible public record of serious intent that can open conversations with research institutions and space agencies.
