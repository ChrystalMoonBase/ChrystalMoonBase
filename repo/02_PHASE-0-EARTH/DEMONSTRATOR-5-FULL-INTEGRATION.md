# Demonstrator 5 — Full System Integration
## Chrystal Moon Base — Phase 0 Earth Programme

**Status:** Not yet begun — requires D1–D4 success  
**Licence:** CC0

---

## Objective

Operate all Phase 0.2 systems together in a representative configuration: power beaming from a fixed mast, four coordinated robots, sintering lunar simulant in the ring pattern, with the Watcher robot transmitting quality control data to a simulated Earth station.

---

## This Is the Pre-Flight Proof of Concept

Demonstrator 5 is as close as Phase 0 can come to simulating the Phase 0.2 Pod operations without going to the Moon. Its purpose is to surface integration problems — issues that do not appear in individual subsystem tests but emerge when everything runs simultaneously.

Demonstrator 5 results, along with the full Phase 0 dataset, will form the scientific record used to approach ESA, NASA, JAXA, and research institutions for Phase 1 partnership discussions.

---

## Test Configuration

- Mast structure: 10–20 metre fixed structure with BSM-equivalent pointing system
- Robots: 4 CMB-R1 prototypes (or closest available to flight configuration)
- Sintering laser: 5–15 kW fibre laser on mast
- Test area: 10 × 10 metre regolith simulant bed
- Data relay: Watcher data transmitted to a simulated Earth station with realistic communication delay (2.5-second simulated delay)
- Duration: minimum 72-hour continuous operation test

---

## Minimum Acceptable Outcome

A 72-hour autonomous sintering run that:
- Produces a measurable sintered area meeting structural quality criteria
- Operates without human intervention beyond monitoring
- Transmits usable Watcher data throughout
- Recovers from at least one simulated fault without stopping

Even a partial success — demonstrating some aspects while revealing specific failures — is a valuable and publishable result.

---

## Publication

All Demonstrator 5 results will be published openly, regardless of outcome. The data, the methods, the failures, and the successes belong to the field — not to CMB alone.
