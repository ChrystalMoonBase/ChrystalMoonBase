# Entertainment Dome
## Chrystal Moon Base — Habitat Concept

**Status:** Concept proposal  
**Licence:** CC0

---

## Overview

Social and recreation dome — the largest domes in the base by interior volume. Open floor plan. Used for communal meals, social gatherings, exercise, cultural events, and any activity requiring a large group in one space. Psychological health of permanent residents depends on these spaces existing.

---

## Shared Architecture

All domes on the Chrystal Moon Base share the same five-layer construction architecture described in DOME-LAYER-ARCHITECTURE.md. There are no wall penetrations in any dome. All connections enter through the floor via the underground network.

---

## Open Questions

This dome type requires detailed interior design work that depends on the specific functions it will house, the equipment that will be installed, and the needs of the residents and tenants. Engineers, interior designers, and end-users familiar with analogous facilities (Antarctic research stations, submarine design, space habitat research) are invited to contribute.
